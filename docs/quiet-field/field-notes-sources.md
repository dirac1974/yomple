**Notes for editors**

- The brief named "Cornell's Bliss copy", but Cornell holds the **Bancroft** copy. The Bliss copy is in the Lincoln Room of the White House (Abraham Lincoln Online, American Battlefield Trust). No note says otherwise.
- The sources give different counts:
  - Burials: 3,354 (American Battlefield Trust), "more than 3,500" (NPS places), 3,564 total Civil War burials (NPS press kit). The notes avoid giving an exact number.
  - Reporters who wrote "under God": three (Battlefield Trust), "at least four" (Constitution Center). The note says only "reporters".
- Some pages could not be opened during checking: the LOC Nicolay transcription page, archives.gov's Declaration transcript and Merriam-Webster. Each fact above rests on the sources listed instead.
