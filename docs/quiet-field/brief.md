# Quiet Field 2 — review brief

**Who:** Sarah, 11, strong student, perfectionism risk, likes depth, mysteries and collecting, heading into pre-algebra. She just **succeeded at Hall of Presidents** (a memory-palace app: walk rooms, each president has an illustrated mnemonic card) and now has to memorize the **Gettysburg Address** (272 words, ~2 min spoken). Her dad says Quiet Field "seems a little boring", "the voice isn't great", "some cuts were a little off", and "anything else to aid the memory because it's a bit long".

**What Quiet Field is now** (live at yomple.com/field/, source `/home/claude/yomple/field/`): a Yomple world (same household/profile system as Hall of Presidents, Bloom, Star Map) for reciting the Gettysburg Address and the Preamble. 12 "lanterns" (phrases) on a dusk path. Steps per lantern: Look (emoji picture, why, gesture, hear it) → Echo (words hidden, optional mic via Web Speech API, "I said it") → Walk (known lanterns in order) → Mix (which line comes first?). Fact states new → practicing → getting solid → shining. Audio = timestamp slices of one public-domain archive.org recitation (Britton Rea) — the slices sometimes clip words ("cuts a little off"); fallback = browser speechSynthesis. Phrase cuts are in `field/phrases.js` (very uneven: 9 to 46 words; lantern 12 holds three big clauses). Code: `field/app.js`, `voice.js`, `index.html`, `styles.css`, `sync.js`, `arrive.js`.

**Siblings for comparison:** Hall of Presidents (what worked for her): `/home/claude/dirac1974/hall-of-presidents/` (README, app.js, play.js, walk.js, parade.js, facts.js, images). Yomple shared rules: `/home/claude/yomple/yomple-shared-implementation.md`. Bloom 2 design principles and expert memos (anxiety-safe, no timers, no red X, typed recall as evidence, ADHD/2e rules) in `/home/claude/bloom/docs/experts/` — same family, same kids.

**Planned voice:** ElevenLabs, a designed (not cloned — no recording of Lincoln exists) voice from historical descriptions: higher, clear, carrying tenor, Kentucky–Indiana accent, measured. Generated once per lantern + full speech, with word timestamps; played offline. Optional atmosphere: open-air reverb, wind, distant crowd, faint crackle. Labelled "an imagined Lincoln".

**Constraints:** static site (GitHub Pages at yomple.com) + Supabase; vanilla JS; works in Safari/Chrome on iPad, phone, laptop; offline after first load; keep the Yomple household/profile/sync system; don't lose any existing progress; the new version will be built at a separate test path (yomple.com/field-next/) before replacing the live one.
