Support

Yomple is a small family of learning games. If something is stuck, start here:

  • Bloom (multiplication): play on the Bloom button from the home page.
  • Another device: on the device that already has the garden, copy the family code, then enter it on the new device. Don’t make a second player with the same name unless you mean to.

More worlds (spelling, presidents) will be linked here as they open.
